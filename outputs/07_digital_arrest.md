## BOT
I'm sorry this happened. **Do these now, in this order:**
1. **Call 1930 (National Cyber Crime Helpline) right now** and also file at **https://cybercrime.gov.in**. Speed matters: the sooner a transaction is reported, the better the chance the receiving account can be put on hold. There is no guarantee.
2. **Call your bank's helpline** (number on the back of your card / official app) and ask them to flag the transaction and block UPI/card if needed. Ask for a complaint/ticket number and note it down.
3. **Do not delete anything.** Keep chats, call logs, screenshots, the payment receipt and the scammer's numbers/IDs.
4. **Do not send more money** to 'release', 'verify' or 'recover' the first payment, and never share your UPI PIN or OTP with anyone. You never need a PIN to *receive* money.

This looks like: **Impersonation of police / agency ('digital arrest') scam**. Government agencies do not 'digitally arrest' anyone or ask for payment over a call. Do not stay on the call; note the number, any badge/ID shown, and any 'case number' they quoted.

To prepare your report I need:
- What is the **12-digit UTR / UPI reference number**? Find it in your payment app under transaction history -> details.
- Which **UPI ID, phone number or account** did the money go to? (e.g. name@bank). Copy it from the transaction details.
(If you don't have one, say so; type **report** any time to get the draft as it is.)

_I am an AI assistant, not a lawyer or the police. I give procedural first-aid and help you organise facts. I cannot guarantee that money will be recovered._
